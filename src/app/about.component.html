Our Mission

Every year, lakhs of Indian students prepare for competitive exams — most without access to quality mentorship, and many while juggling a job or final-year college. MentorStreak exists to level that field: structured courses, exam-pattern mocks with real analysis, and a 24/7 AI mentor, at a price that doesn't require a loan. We launched with CAT — structured QA, VARC and DILR preparation — and more courses are on the way.

Our Vision

We believe the next generation of IIM admits won't come only from metro coaching hubs. With personalised AI mentorship, smart revision systems, and disciplined daily streaks, an aspirant in a tier-3 town can prepare with the same rigour as one sitting in a Delhi classroom — often better.
